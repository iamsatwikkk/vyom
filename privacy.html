Simple, honest, and written in plain language.

Our commitment ✦

VYOM was built on a simple belief: a browser homepage should serve you, not advertisers, not data brokers, and not anyone else. This page explains exactly what data VYOM collects (almost none), what happens to the data it does touch, and what your rights are as a user.

Short version: VYOM does not collect your search queries, does not use tracking cookies, does not sell data, and does not serve advertisements. What you search is your business alone.

Search queries ✦

When you type a query into VYOM and press search, your query is sent directly to the search engine you have selected — Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Yahoo, or Ecosia. VYOM acts purely as a launcher; it does not intercept, read, store, or transmit your query to any VYOM server, because there is no VYOM server in that path.

Each search engine operates under its own privacy policy. If you are concerned about what a particular engine knows about you, choosing DuckDuckGo or Brave Search from the tab switcher offers significantly stronger privacy guarantees — neither links searches to a personal profile.

Cookies & local storage ✦

VYOM does not set any tracking cookies. The only browser storage VYOM may use is lightweight session memory to remember which search engine tab you last selected — this data never leaves your device and is cleared when your browser session ends. No cookie banners, no consent walls, because there is nothing to consent to.

Third-party services ✦

VYOM loads Google Fonts (for typography) from Google's CDN. This means Google's servers receive a standard request for the font file, which may include your IP address and browser information — as is the case with any website that loads a resource from a third-party host. VYOM does not control what Google logs from that request.

Favicon images for search engines (the small icons next to each engine tab) are fetched from those engines' own domains. This is a lightweight, standard request and does not transmit any query or personal data.

VYOM+ (when launched) will use OpenAI GPT as its AI backend — a third-party service with its own privacy policy. Use of VYOM+ will be explicitly opt-in, and you will be shown OpenAI's data handling terms before activating it.

Contact & queries ✦

When you use the FAQ contact form, your email address and message are passed to your local mail client via a standard mailto: link. VYOM does not transmit this data through any server — the data goes wherever your mail client sends it. The recipient is the creator, Satwik, at a personal email address used solely to respond to genuine enquiries.

Your rights ✦

Because VYOM does not collect personal data, there is nothing to request, correct, export, or delete from any VYOM database — such a database does not exist. If you have privacy concerns about a specific search engine's data practices, contact that engine directly; their privacy teams can fulfil data subject requests under GDPR, CCPA, and equivalent legislation.

If you believe something on this page is inaccurate or have a specific privacy question, please reach out via the FAQ page.

Changes to this policy ✦

This policy reflects VYOM as it exists today. If the project evolves — particularly around VYOM+ — this page will be updated and the effective date will be noted at the top. VYOM will never introduce data collection silently; any meaningful change will be communicated clearly.
