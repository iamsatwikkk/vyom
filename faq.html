Everything you need to know about VYOM.

VYOM is a browser homepage that lets you switch between multiple search engines — Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Yahoo and Ecosia — from a single, beautiful interface. The name comes from the Sanskrit word for sky.

Click any engine tab above the search bar. Your selection is applied immediately. You can also press / anywhere on the homepage to jump straight to the search field.

VYOM+ is an upcoming AI-powered assistant built into the browser. It is powered by OpenAI GPT — a third-party service — and is not developed or owned by VYOM or Satwik. VYOM+ is currently in development; stay tuned.

No. VYOM does not store, log, or transmit your search queries. Your searches go directly to the engine you select. For maximum privacy, choose DuckDuckGo or Brave — neither builds a search profile on you.

VYOM does not set any tracking cookies. Minor session memory may be used to remember your last selected engine — this never leaves your device and is cleared when your session ends.

Yes — the core VYOM experience is completely free, with no ads and no hidden costs. VYOM+ (the AI assistant) will be a premium add-on once it launches.

VYOM was designed and built by Satwik. Visit the About page to learn more about the project and the person behind it.
